Our Team

Luunja Secondary School (Estonia)

Luunja Secondary School is a municipal school located 10 kilometers from Luunja District, near Tartu, Estonia’s second largest city. With a history dating back to 1736, the school has evolved through wars and reconstructions to provide both primary and secondary education today. The school is equipped with a health room, library, science lab, cafeteria, and computer labs. Students benefit from advanced science education and extensive access to digital tools.

Agrupamento de Escolas Terras do Ave (Portugal)

Located in Vila Nova de Famalicão, Braga, AETA was established in 2000–2001 and restructured in 2007–2008. It currently covers 10 educational units and is part of the Priority Intervention Educational Territory Program (TEIP). With a strong focus on inclusion, innovation, and technology, the school promotes clubs, coding, robotics, and Erasmus+ projects, fostering active student participation and modern learning practices.

Aydın Ticaret Borsası (Turkey)

Aydın Ticaret Borsası provides specialized education for gifted and talented students outside regular school hours. With 36 teachers and 715 students, the center identifies children with exceptional mental, artistic, or musical abilities and offers enriched education programs. Its curriculum covers adaptation, supportive education, talent recognition, special talent development, and project management.

Mlyst (Finland)

Mlyst is a game development company with expertise in creating digital tools, websites, apps, and games. Beyond development, Mlyst provides educational resources and training in game design and digital technologies, leveraging its experience to support both commercial and educational projects.
